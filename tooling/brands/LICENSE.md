# Brand logo provenance & trademark note

The SVG marks under `svg/` are vendored so TonalDepth can render the logos of
the products it and its consumers work with (Medusa, ERPNext, Frappe, …). The
**path data** is freely reusable; the **marks themselves are trademarks of their
respective owners**. They are included for nominative, first-party use — showing
which tools a product integrates with — not to imply endorsement.

| slug | mark | source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `medusa` | Medusa | user-supplied (single-path, monochrome) |
| `erpnext` | ERPNext | thesvg.org / simple-icons format |
| `frappe` | Frappe | thesvg.org / simple-icons format |

`thesvg.org` publishes ~6,500 brand SVGs; new marks are curated in by hand into
`svg/` + `manifest.json` — there is no live/runtime dependency on it.
